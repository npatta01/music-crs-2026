What is not ideal

  • We tuned on what we measured.The development set was both our training and our evaluation data, and the response prompt was picked against the Blind-A leaderboard: 4.70 there, 3.30 on Blind-B.
  • We extracted more than we used.Most fields in the state never reached retrieval or ranking at all.
  • Nothing from outside the catalog.No outside source for what a track or artist is known for, and no tempo, key or soundtrack. Past one CF vector, no listening behaviour.
  • The reply was never checked.Asked for a track we do not have, it described another by the same artist.
